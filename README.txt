Product Name:
uGo

Product Description:
uGo is a carpooling website for University of Ottawa students and staff. It allows users to offer or find rides based on their destination, date, and time.

Purpose:
The goal of uGo is to make transportation easier to access by connecting people who need rides with people who can provide them.

Target Audience:
University of Ottawa students and staff.

Key Features:
- Post available rides
- Search for rides by location, date, and time
- View ride duration
- Book rides in advance
- Simple and easy-to-navigate interface

Value Proposition:
uGo provides a free transportation option that can make travelling to and from university more convenient, including for students who need rides at night.
